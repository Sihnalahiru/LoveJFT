              💡 JFT Basic Study Tips

              ✅ JFT Basic tests 4 sections: Script/Vocabulary, Listening 1, Listening 2, and Reading
              ✅ Passing score: Around 200/250 points (80%). Each section has a minimum threshold.
              ✅ Focus on hiragana/katakana first - the test uses Japanese scripts throughout
              ✅ Practice listening daily - the listening sections account for half the exam
              ✅ Use spaced repetition for vocabulary (Anki, Duolingo, or our flashcard tool)
              ✅ Take mock tests in the final weeks to simulate exam conditions
